PART A: JEDCO OFFICIAL EVALUATION METHODOLOGY
(Source: الالية_العامة — applies to ALL programs)
================================================================

A.1 THREE EVALUATION STAGES (used for all programs)
------------------------------------------------------
Stage 1: ADMINISTRATIVE & FINANCIAL EVALUATION
  - Verify application meets program eligibility conditions
  - Verify company eligibility (sector, size, ownership, age)
  - Verify financial support request eligibility
  - Verify project aligns with program objectives
  - Analyze company financial position using audited statements
  - Processing rate: 33 applications/working day (admin check)
    (source: JEDCO official evaluation methodology)
                     13 applications/working day (financial eval)

Stage 2: TECHNICAL EVALUATION
  - Assess degree to which project aligns with program objectives
  - Analyze export/market opportunities in target countries
  - Review project description and opportunity-seeking approach
  - Evaluate sustainability and impact (company level + national)
  - TWO evaluators assess each application independently
  - They meet to discuss, reconcile marks, and flag differences
  - Applications with major scoring discrepancies are re-evaluated
  - Processing rate: 2 applications/working day

Stage 3: FIELD VISITS
  - Visit company premises to verify declared data
  - Review company past performance: sales growth, production capacity
  - Inspect facilities and production lines
  - Review the export product for market fitness
  - Interview sales & marketing team: English, computer skills, experience
  - Check promotional materials and current tools
  - Processing rate: 2 visits/working day

Stage 4: FINAL BUDGET REVIEW & CONTRACT PREPARATION
  - Technical evaluator reviews project budget for program compliance
  - All line items checked for eligibility
  - Budget totals verified for arithmetic errors
  - Final audit by Grants Department + Internal Control
  - Contracts prepared: include application form, budget, procurement rules

A.2 EVALUATION COMMITTEE STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------
- Chaired by: JEDCO Executive Director
- Members: Internal Control representative
- Admin/Financial Evaluation Committee: teams of 2 evaluators per application
- Technical Evaluation Committee: teams of 2 engineers per application

A.3 FINAL SCORING LOGIC
------------------------------------------------------
- Each technical evaluator scores independently
- Consensus score = average of the two evaluators' scores
- Final technical score = consensus score + financial evaluation score
- Applications ranked from highest to lowest score
- Minimum passing score threshold set based on:
  * Number of applications received
  * Available budget envelope
  * Overall quality of the applicant pool

PART B: EXPORT SUPPORT PROGRAM (دعم الشركات الصناعية للتصدير)
(Most detailed criteria available — model for all programs)
================================================================

B.1 PROGRAM OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------
Full name: برنامج دعم الشركات الصناعية من أجل التصدير لأول مرة
           (Industrial Export Support — First Time Exporters)
Director: د. بشار الزعبي, Industry Support Directorate
Vision: Create a competitive Jordanian industrial sector in global markets

B.2 GRANT FINANCIAL TERMS
------------------------------------------------------
Grant ceiling:         40,000 JOD per company
Funding ratio:         Max 70% of total project cost
  CONSTRAINT: JEDCO's share of buildings + equipment + supplies
              must NOT exceed 20,000 JOD and NOT exceed 50% of total grant
Payment Schedule:
  First installment:   80% of grant — paid UPFRONT at contract signing
                       (against court guarantee + bank cheque for full amount)
  Final installment:   20% of grant — paid at project closure after
                       all activities completed and financial closure done

B.3 ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS (HARD DISQUALIFIERS)
------------------------------------------------------
A company is INELIGIBLE if ANY of the following apply:

  ✗ Not an industrial company
  ✗ Not registered with Ministry of Industry, Trade & Supply
  ✗ IS a public shareholding company (شركة مساهمة عامة)
  ✗ Company age < 2 years before application date
  ✗ Ownership is NOT 100% private sector
  ✗ Value-added of main products < 35% (per Ministry calculation)
     (exception: companies that have never exported before)
  ✗ Employees < 5 or > 250 (per Social Security records at time of application)
  ✗ Company lacks financial viability (profitability + liquidity)
     per last 2 years of audited financial statements
  ✗ For pharma/cosmetics/food/chemical sectors:
     not registered with Jordan Food & Drug Administration
  ✗ Previously benefited from this program in Rounds 1 or 2

PRIORITY (not disqualifying, but boosts ranking):
  ★ Company has NEVER exported before (given highest priority)
  ★ Annual exports < 200,000 JOD in any of last 3 years
  ★ Total sales between 200,000 – 3,000,000 JOD (2019 reference year)

B.4 ELIGIBLE ACTIVITIES (WHAT THE GRANT COVERS)
------------------------------------------------------
CAPACITY BUILDING (Productive):
  ✓ Machinery, equipment, devices
  ✓ Computer equipment and computerized systems
  ✓ Rental of offices/distribution centers/warehouses in export markets
  ✓ Prototypes of new products
  ✓ Sample shipments (max 150 kg / target export country)
  ✓ Shipping costs and insurance for first export order

ADMINISTRATIVE CAPACITY BUILDING:
  ✓ Promotional materials: brochures, packaging design, label design
  ✓ Consulting services: training, coaching, export coaching, market studies
  ✓ B2B meeting organization
  ✓ Product registration in target export countries
  ✓ International trade fair participation (booth + sample shipping)
  ✓ E-store creation and digital campaigns
  ✓ Technical certifications
  ✓ Travel for marketing in target markets

B.5 INELIGIBLE COSTS (HARD EXCLUSIONS FROM BUDGET)
------------------------------------------------------
  ✗ Any cost incurred BEFORE contract signing date
  ✗ Any cost incurred AFTER contract end date
  ✗ Currency exchange losses
  ✗ Taxes and government fees (customs stamps only)
  ✗ Salaries / payroll
  ✗ Raw materials (except materials used in manufacturing prototype products)
  ✗ Furniture and vehicles
  ✗ Consulting fees for managing the grant itself
  ✗ Land, building purchases, and vehicle purchases
  ✗ Promotional items: pens, mugs, agendas, etc.
  ✗ Provisions for future potential losses or liabilities
  ✗ Costs already covered by another program or project

B.6 REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (3 paper copies + 1 electronic each)
------------------------------------------------------
  □ Company registration certificate (Ministry of Industry)
      — must show founding date and authorized signatories
  □ Valid operating license (رخصة مهن سارية المفعول)
  □ Customs declarations for top 3 export products
      (waived for companies that have never exported)
  □ Social Security enrollment record (last 3 months)
  □ Audited financial statements — last 2 fiscal years
  □ Company national ID copy
  □ Export plan (خطة تصدير)
  □ Food & Drug Administration registration (pharma/food/cosmetics only)

B.7 APPLICATION PROCESS (TWO PHASES)
------------------------------------------------------
Phase 1: Export Catalyst Camp (مخيم تحفيز التصدير)
  - Online application
  - Training sessions covering:
    * Export readiness self-assessment
    * Identifying international market opportunities
    * Packaging, labeling, pricing, shipping, Incoterms
    * HS codes, rules of origin, value-added calculation
    * Tariffs, technical certifications
    * EU Pan-Euro-Med rules of origin agreement
    * Export financing, insurance, managing overseas payments
    * Preparing an export plan
  - Only companies passing Phase 1 are invited to Phase 2

Phase 2: Grant Application
  - Submit detailed project proposal + budget
  - Submit all required documents
  - Full technical + financial + field evaluation

B.8 TIMELINE
------------------------------------------------------
Promotional activities (launch):               2 months
Service contracting + e-form development:      2 months
Application intake period:                     2 months from launch event
Training sessions (camp):                      1 week
Phase 2 application intake:                    2 months
Evaluation and selection:                      per budget/applications volume
Implementation period per company:            3 months minimum – 12 months maximum

PART C: TATTWEER PROGRAM (برنامج تطوير)
(Business Development / Existing SME Expansion)
================================================================

C.1 OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------
Target: Existing Jordanian SMEs seeking to expand or modernize
Grant ceiling: Up to 50,000 JOD
Funding ratio: 70% of total project cost (applicant covers 30%)
Implementation period: 12 months from contract signing

C.2 KEY ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
------------------------------------------------------
  ✓ Existing registered business (not a new startup)
  ✓ Jordanian-majority ownership
  ✗ Public shareholding companies excluded
  ✓ Operating license must be valid throughout implementation period
     (12 months from contract signing — CRITICAL compliance check)
  ✓ Financial viability demonstrated via audited statements
  ✓ Must not have benefited from the same program previously

OPERATING LICENSE TRAP:
  If contract signed September 2026 → project ends September 2027
  License expiring December 2026 → LICENSE EXPIRES DURING PROJECT
  This means the application does not meet this condition — must be classified as ineligible or license renewed first

C.3 ELIGIBLE ACTIVITIES
------------------------------------------------------
  ✓ Machinery and production equipment upgrades
  ✓ ERP / business management systems
  ✓ Quality certifications (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, etc.)
  ✓ Product development
  ✓ Market access and export readiness investments
  ✓ Staff training (technical and managerial)

C.4 REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
------------------------------------------------------
  □ Commercial registration certificate
  □ Valid operating license (رخصة مهن)
  □ Audited financial statements (last 2 years)
  □ Detailed project proposal and action plan
  □ Budget breakdown
  □ Social Security employee records

PART D: START BUSINESS PROGRAM (اعمل مشروعك)
(New Business Startup Grants)
================================================================

D.1 OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------
Target: New entrepreneurs starting their first business
Grant: Non-refundable grant (منحة غير مستردة)
Grant ceiling: Up to 35,000 JOD
Funding ratio: 70% (entrepreneur covers 30%)
Directorate: New Enterprises Directorate (مديرية المشاريع الناشئة)
Location: Can be submitted at JEDCO branches in:
  Amman, Irbid, Ajloun, Madaba, Karak, Mafraq, and Ma'an

D.2 KEY ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
------------------------------------------------------
  ✓ Jordanian nationality
  ✓ Age 18 or older
  ✓ Has a viable and economically feasible business idea
  ✓ Has academic/technical qualifications in the project field
  ✓ Must attend "Start Your Own Business" training course
  ✓ Project to be implemented in any governorate
  ✓ Project must be productive or service-oriented
  ✗ Purely commercial/retail projects excluded
  ✓ Enterprise must be < 2 years old at time of application

D.3 REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (at application stage)
------------------------------------------------------
  □ Business incubator enrollment application
  □ Business plan (خطة عمل)
  □ Financial disbursement schedule (جدول الصرف المالي)
  □ CV / résumé
  □ National ID

POST-GRANT DOCUMENTS (after approval):
  □ Business registration at Ministry of Industry & Trade
     (must be registered in the beneficiary's name)
  □ Dedicated bank account for the grant + chequebook
  □ Court guarantee (كفالة عدلية)

D.4 APPLICATION WINDOWS
------------------------------------------------------
Last week of: April, August, and December each year

D.5 PROCESSING TIMELINE
------------------------------------------------------
Evaluation completion: under 2 months from application deadline
During implementation (1 year):
  - Disbursement requests submitted monthly from the 1st
  - Financial transactions completed within 14 working days

PART E: TAMKEEN PROGRAM (تمكين)
(Employment Support)
================================================================

E.1 OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------
Target: Companies creating new Jordanian employment
Focus: Subsidizing wages and training costs to reduce unemployment
Geographic coverage: All Jordan governorates

E.2 KEY ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
------------------------------------------------------
  ✓ Company registered and operating legally
  ✓ Committed to hiring Jordanian nationals
  ✓ Salary for supported employees at or above minimum wage
  ✓ Employee enrolled in Social Security within 30 days of hiring
  ✓ Company not in arrears with Social Security or tax authorities

E.3 SUPPORT STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------
  - Wage subsidy for newly hired Jordanians (partial, time-limited)
  - Training costs coverage for new hires
  - Duration: typically 6-12 months per employee

PART I: CROSS-PROGRAM AUTOMATION ANALYSIS
(What AI can check automatically vs. what requires human review)
================================================================

FULLY AUTOMATABLE (rule-based, deterministic):
  ✓ Document completeness check (present vs. required list)
  ✓ Operating license expiry date vs. project end date
  ✓ Company type exclusion (public shareholding)
  ✓ Employee count range check (5–250 for Export program)
  ✓ Grant amount calculation (70% of cost, subject to ceiling)
  ✓ Ineligible cost line items in budget
  ✓ Submission window compliance (application received in window?)
  ✓ Duplicate application check (same company, same program, same cycle)
  ✓ Company age check (< 2 years = ineligible for Tattweer)

AI-ASSISTED (LLM analysis, human confirms):
  ✓ Sector classification → priority vs. low-priority vs. excluded
  ✓ Value-added estimation from business description
  ✓ Financial viability assessment from stated revenue/employees
  ✓ Export potential assessment (Export program)
  ✓ Business plan quality scoring
  ✓ Project-program alignment scoring
  ✓ Risk flag identification (vague milestones, unrealistic budgets)

HUMAN ONLY (institutional judgment, legal accountability):
  ✗ Final INVITE/REJECT decision for borderline cases
  ✗ Field visit scheduling and conduct
  ✗ Contract negotiation and modification
  ✗ Budget line-item negotiation
  ✗ Committee deliberation on appeals
