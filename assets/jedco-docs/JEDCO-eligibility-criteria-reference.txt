1. START BUSINESS — اعمل مشروعك
   For: NEW startups (individuals, not existing businesses)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------
   TARGET:         Jordanian individuals aged 21+ from any governorate
   SECTORS:        Industry, crafts, agriculture, services — all governorates
   PRIORITY:       High-value 5 sectors / tech & digital / tourism / agri / creative
   GRANT AMOUNT:   Up to 50,000 JOD (non-refundable)
   JEDCO SHARE:    Up to 70% of total project cost
   APPLICANT SHARE: Minimum 30% of total project cost
   TIMELINE:       1 year from signing agreement
   ANNUAL TARGET:  40 projects per year
   TOTAL BUDGET:   2,000,000 JOD/year (government funded)
   APPLICATION:    Sept 1 – Oct 31 each year (online only)

   ELIGIBILITY CHECKLIST:
   [✓] Jordanian nationality, age 21+
   [✓] Relevant experience/knowledge in proposed business field
   [✓] Financial capacity to cover 30% share (proven by bank statement)
   [✓] Business idea not yet registered (new startup only)

   REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
   - Online application form (via jedco.gov.jo portal)
   - Copy of national ID
   - Detailed budget with Action Plan (timetable)
   - Proof of financial capacity:
     * Bank statement (last 3 months) OR
     * Property ownership document OR
     * Personal guarantee from first-degree relative
   - (Commercial registration — obtained AFTER initial approval)

   APPROVED COSTS:
   ✓ Site rental costs
   ✓ Infrastructure development
   ✓ Machinery, production lines, equipment generating income
   ✓ Interior/exterior fit-out
   ✓ Installation and maintenance of equipment
   ✓ Packaging and labeling
   ✓ E-commerce setup and digital marketing tools
   ✓ Transport vehicles (directly linked to project)

   EXCLUDED COSTS:
   ✗ Permanent employee salaries
   ✗ Land/building acquisition
   ✗ Loan repayments
   ✗ Raw materials
   ✗ All taxes (income, customs, sales tax)
   ✗ Costs incurred before signing the agreement

   SELECTION PRIORITIES:
   → Projects with technology/innovation component
   → Projects with high employment creation potential
   → Projects tied to each governorate's comparative advantage

2. TATTWEER — تطوير
   For: EXISTING SMEs (already operating businesses)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------
   TARGET:         Existing micro, small, and medium enterprises
   SECTORS:        Industrial, service, craft, agricultural — all Jordan
   PRIORITY:       High-value 5 sectors, tech/digital, green economy
   GRANT AMOUNT:   Up to 50,000 JOD (non-refundable)
   JEDCO SHARE:    Up to 70% of total eligible project cost
   APPLICANT SHARE: Minimum 30%
   TIMELINE:       Maximum 1 year from signing
   ANNUAL TARGET:  20+ projects per year
   TOTAL BUDGET:   1,000,000 JOD/year (government funded)
   APPLICATION:    Sept 1 – Oct 31 (online only)

   ELIGIBILITY CHECKLIST:
   [✓] Registered with official Jordanian authorities
   [✓] Privately owned (must be private sector)
   [✓] Classified as MSME (micro/small/medium enterprise)
   [✓] JEDCO share request does not exceed 70% of total project cost
   [✓] Business is in an eligible sector (no tobacco, alcohol, cement, mining)

   REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
   - Online application form
   - Detailed budget with Action Plan timetable
   - Updated commercial registration (showing establishment date, partners, signatories)
   - AUDITED financial statements for last TWO fiscal years
   - Bank account statement (last 6 months)
   - 3 comparative price quotes for major equipment
   - Valid operating license from relevant authority

   APPROVED COSTS:
   ✓ Computer systems and software (ERP, CRM, etc.)
   ✓ Operational/marketing/admin automation
   ✓ Transport vehicles and operational equipment
   ✓ Energy-saving and waste treatment systems
   ✓ Project infrastructure development
   ✓ Digital transformation and e-commerce
   ✓ Green economy initiatives
   ✓ Packaging and design improvements
   ✓ Quality certifications (ISO, etc.)
   ✓ Workforce training tied to the project

   EXCLUDED COSTS:
   ✗ Loss provisions or contingent liabilities
   ✗ Permanent employee salaries and wages
   ✗ Accrued interest
   ✗ Existing loan repayments
   ✗ Costs incurred before signing the agreement
   ✗ Currency exchange losses
   ✗ Raw materials
   ✗ All taxes

   SELECTION PRIORITIES:
   → Projects with high economic or environmental impact
   → Innovative/creative projects
   → Projects providing competitive advantage in the local region

3. TAMKEEN — تمكين (CURRENTLY INACTIVE — previous round ended 2024)
   For: Existing SMEs — technology & green economy focus
   ----------------------------------------------------------------
   TARGET:         MSMEs across all economic sectors; all Jordan governorates
   EXCLUDED:       Tobacco, alcohol, cement, mining industries
   MIN EMPLOYEES:  5 staff minimum
   GRANT AMOUNT:   Up to 50,000 JOD (non-refundable)
   JEDCO SHARE:    Up to 70%
   APPLICANT SHARE: Minimum 30%
   TIMELINE:       12 months from signing
   TARGET COUNT:   30 projects in 2024

   SPECIAL FOCUS:
   - Smart/emerging technologies (AR, VR, blockchain, IoT, AI)
   - Green economy transition
   - Digital transformation of operations

   OWNERSHIP CONDITION: 75%+ privately owned

   REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (similar to Tattweer, plus):
   - Updated commercial registration
   - Detailed budget
   - Proof of financial capacity
   - (No audited financials explicitly required — lighter documentation)

COMMON RULES ACROSS ALL PROGRAMS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- All applications are ONLINE ONLY via jedco.gov.jo portal
- No paper/PDF application forms issued by JEDCO
- All grants are NON-REFUNDABLE (منحة غير مستردة)
- JEDCO covers max 70% — applicant MUST contribute minimum 30%
- Maximum grant per project: 50,000 JOD
- Implementation period: maximum 12 months from agreement signing
- Field visits may be conducted as part of the review process
- Results announced on a rolling basis as applications are evaluated

REVIEW PROCESS (all programs):
1. Official announcement + awareness workshops
2. Online application portal opens
3. JEDCO receives applications electronically
4. Administrative review (documents completeness check)
5. Technical evaluation committee review
6. Field visits (if required)
7. Announcement of selected projects
8. Agreement signing with beneficiaries
9. Implementation begins
10. Progress monitoring + final disbursement
